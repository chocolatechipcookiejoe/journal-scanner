    iPhone and laptop

    1. Point the iPhone's Camera app at this code and tap the link. It opens the scanner in Safari.
    2. First time only, Safari warns that the connection isn't private: tap Show Details, then visit this website. (To stop the warning for good, tap Install certificate on the phone afterwards.)
    3. Tap Allow for the camera, put the phone on its stand over the journal, and flip.
    Narrating from the laptop?

    Copy this link here, then paste it into Safari on your laptop (the copy reaches it through Universal Clipboard). It opens Narrate there. First time only, Safari warns the connection isn't private: click Show Details → visit this website → Visit Website, and enter the laptop's password. That makes the laptop trust this Mac for good, so the microphone works.

    Pages travel from the phone to this Mac over your home Wi-Fi, encrypted. Nothing goes to the internet. The code is a private key to your scanner; don't share a photo of it.

    Settings

    Off: pages are only saved, and wait in line. Turn it back on to read them all. Install certificate (stops Safari's warning) After downloading: Settings → Profile Downloaded → Install. Then Settings → General → About → Certificate Trust Settings → turn on "Journal Scanner".
